Value before friction

  • Code entry is still one tap away, demoted to a quiet link.

Sell the payoff

Apple Sign In stays the App Store requirement — but now it's framed by what the user gets.

04 — Home · the storefront

What matters, above the fold

The headline change. The old "Order" tab listed delivery dates; this answers who needs feeding and by when.

  • Next-up card — the single most urgent action, with a live cutoff countdown in clay.
  • Your eaters — children become the lens; tap to filter the whole home to one child.
  • Weekly module is permanent — a progress-tracked card that never disappears (fixes the vanishing banner).
  • One-tap reorder — the biggest retention lever in food apps, drawn from order history.
  • Dates demoted to a section, now photo-forward. New 4th tab: Weekly.

A summary, not a form

08 — Weekly plan

Promoted to a destination

The platform's highest-value feature — finally a tab, reachable in every state. The screen was already strong; it gets the chassis and imagery.

  • Progress header — "3 of 4 days planned" sets a clear goal.
  • Schedule-driven slots — renders only the days the operator actually runs (Mon–Thu here), no hardcoded "5 days".
  • Food thumbnails per slot; sticky one-tap weekly checkout.

09 — Success

End on momentum

The old success screen celebrated, then dead-ended at "Back to menu". This carries the user into the next valuable action.

  • "Finish the week" — turns one order into a full weekly plan.
  • "Order for another eater" — multi-child households are the norm; nudge it.
  • Keeps the celebration + order ref; loses the dead end.
